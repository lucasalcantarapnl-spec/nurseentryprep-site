Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Nurse Entry Prep ("we") sells independent study materials for nursing entrance exams. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Diagnostic answers: your answers to the free diagnostic are processed in your browser to show your score. We do not store them on a server.
  • Purchase data: payments are processed by our payment partner, Hotmart, which collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name and email to deliver your purchase and provide support. We never see your full card number.
  • Analytics and advertising: we use Google Analytics / Google Ads tags and cookies to measure visits and which ads lead to purchases. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Emails you send us: if you contact support, we keep the conversation to help you.

How we use it

To deliver your purchase, answer support requests, process refunds, prevent fraud, and improve our materials and ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your personal data by emailing support@nurseentryprep.com. California residents may exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA the same way.

Children

Our products are intended for adults preparing for nursing school admission and are not directed to children under 13.
